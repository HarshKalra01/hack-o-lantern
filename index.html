Chapter I

The Town Above

Every 27 years, something wakes up under Derry. It sleeps in the storm drains, curled between the pipes, and when it opens its eyes it is hungry.

The townsfolk never talk about it. They just keep their kids away from the gutters when it rains, and they don't ask why the drains smell like popcorn and rust.

"Come down here... it's warm in the dark."

Chapter II

The Hunger

Pennywise doesn't just hunt. He feeds on fear. A shriek at a sleepover. A heartbeat skipping on a dark staircase. A scream echoing down a pipe is a feast that keeps him grinning for decades.

A red balloon bobbing where no balloon should be. A face in the drain. For centuries Derry fed him, cycle after cycle. And he was happy.

Did you hear something behind you?

Chapter III

The Fading

But this time, when he woke, Derry had changed. The kids had glowing screens now, full of things scarier than any drain. They laughed at the dark. They walked right past the gutters.

He held out a balloon, the way he always had. Nobody took it. And Pennywise noticed something horrible: without their fear, he was disappearing.

"Why did you stop being scared?"

The Dilemma

His Hunger

If he scares you, he lives. He grows fat on it and sleeps another 27 years. The drains get louder, the balloons come back... and he stays a monster, because nobody gets close to a monster.

His Loneliness

If he stops, he fades away for good. But for one moment before he's gone, someone might sit by the drain and talk to him. Not scream. Talk.

A clown who can only exist if you're afraid of him.
